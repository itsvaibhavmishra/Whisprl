# Temporary changelog - x.x.x
#
# Every pull request into rc adds its line here, under the heading that matches who the
# change is for. Releasing empties this file into CHANGELOG.md under the new version, so
# nothing has to be remembered or reconstructed from commit messages at release time.
#
#   New          something you could not do before
#   Improvements something you could do, now better
#   Fixed        something that was wrong
#   Developers   anything that changes how the app is built or tested
#   Internal     refactors and housekeeping nobody outside would notice
#
# Headings sit at two spaces, entries at four. release-ready.yml relies on that difference to
# tell an entry from an empty heading, so keep the indentation as it is.

  - New:
    - Talk at the table: on a phone a game gets its own bar for saying something and throwing emoji, players' words show beside their seat and everyone else's drift across the game, and messages typed during a game say which game in the chat

  - Improvements:
    - A game's rules open as pages inside the game, each with a picture drawn in that game's own style
    - A game's leaderboard opens inside the game and shows only that game: a head to head for two players, and a podium with everyone's standings for more

  - Fixed:
    - The status bar's clock and icons follow Whisprl's own light or dark mode, and stay light over the photo editor and viewers
    - The loading spinner sits in the middle of what you can see, and the page no longer jumps while it shows
    - A two or three finger tap while drawing makes its line or circle even when the first finger drifts as it lands

  - Developers:
    - An app release also attaches the Play bundle, whisprl-play.aab, ready to upload to the Play Console
    - An app release's test build talks to the rc backend on Render, which deploys at the same commit only for that build

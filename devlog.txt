# Temporary changelog - x.x.x
#
# Every pull request into rc adds its line here, under the heading that matches who the
# change is for. Releasing empties this file into CHANGELOG.md under the new version, so
# nothing has to be remembered or reconstructed from commit messages at release time.
#
#   New          something you could not do before
#   Improvements something you could do, now better
#   Fixed        something that was wrong
#   Developers   anything that changes how the app is built or tested
#   Internal     refactors and housekeeping nobody outside would notice
#
# Headings sit at two spaces, entries at four. release-ready.yml relies on that difference to
# tell an entry from an empty heading, so keep the indentation as it is.

  - New:
    - Setup asks for gender (male, female or prefer not to say), shown on your profile unless you prefer not to say; once set, only support can change it

  - Improvements:
    - Whisprl is now Whisprl: Chat & Games: the welcome page opens on a game being dealt in a chat and the real cards of every game, and page titles, the link preview image, the web app's name, emails and the terms and privacy pages call it that
    - A birthday can be changed once a year, and never in a way that moves you between under 18 and adult
    - Jackaroo previews a move as lit dots in each hole it steps through and a ring where it lands, glows softly round each marble you can pick, draws the marbles a little smaller, and always lets you pick both marbles for a jack and how a seven is shared
    - Signing in is one tap with Google, on the website and in the app, on a single screen: email sign up and its emailed code, passwords, GitHub, LinkedIn and passkey sign in are gone, passkeys still unlock your messages, and Settings, Security can log out your other devices

  - Fixed:
    - Two against two Jackaroo: a marble can jump one marble, yours included, and land on the next to knock it out; a seven can move either marble first; your partner's marble in front of your home no longer shuts it; the five shows all four colours; numbers on crowded marbles stay on screen; the home rings sit centred; and the sleeping cat's eyes no longer sparkle

  - Developers:
    - The backend's REVIEW_ACCOUNT_EMAIL and REVIEW_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD give Play's reviewers the one password sign in, behind App review sign in in the app; the GitHub, LinkedIn and reCAPTCHA settings are no longer read
